# Small Warehouse Layout and Labeling Plan

This plan is designed for a remodeling contractor handling several simultaneous high-end kitchen and bathroom projects. Adjust physical dimensions to the actual building, but preserve the control zones and one-way material flow.

## Recommended material flow

**Truck arrival → Receiving/inspection → Accepted storage or quarantine → Project staging → Field loadout → Return/surplus**

Avoid moving material directly from the truck into permanent storage before receiving is completed.

## Core zones

### RCV — Receiving and inspection

Purpose:

- Temporary unloading
- BOL, PO, packing-slip, count, label, and visible-condition verification
- Photo capture
- Driver exception documentation

Rules:

- No material remains overnight unless tagged and assigned
- Floor markings separate unverified, accepted, and rejected loads
- Mobile inspection station has charging, lighting, measuring tools, label printer, and photo backdrop

### QTN — Quarantine and claims hold

Purpose:

- Damaged, wrong-item, wrong-finish, lot-mismatch, or unverified material

Rules:

- Red claim/hold label
- Physical separation from accepted material
- Claim number, project, vendor, owner, deadline, and disposition displayed
- No field issue without authorized release

### CAB — Climate-controlled cabinetry and millwork

Purpose:

- Cabinets, finished panels, fillers, moldings, vanities, and finished wood components

Rules:

- Dry, temperature-stable, padded, and protected from stacking or impact
- Cabinet tags visible without moving the product
- Group by project, room, and elevation

### STN — Stone and slab A-frame area

Purpose:

- Natural stone, quartz, porcelain slabs, and fabricated pieces

Rules:

- Approved A-frame/upright support and restraints
- Bundle/slab IDs visible
- No forklift or pallet traffic through the protected footprint
- Damage/condition photos before and after every movement

### TIL — Tile and palletized finish material

Purpose:

- Tile, mosaics, trims, waterproofing, grout, and setting materials

Rules:

- Keep dry
- Lot, shade, caliber, and room allocation labels visible
- Do not mix lots in the same staging group without approval
- Shelf-life items rotated and expiration-controlled

### PLB — Plumbing fixtures and oversize protected storage

Purpose:

- Tubs, sinks, toilets, vanities, shower bases, and large fixtures

Rules:

- Padded floor zones and manufacturer-required orientation
- No stacking on decorative surfaces
- Protective film and packaging retained when appropriate

### SEC — Secure high-value cage

Purpose:

- Faucets, trim kits, controls, hardware, lighting controls, appliance accessories, and other small high-value material

Rules:

- Restricted access
- Scan or transaction required for entry and removal
- Cycle-count high-value items more frequently

### APP — Appliance and equipment area

Purpose:

- Appliances, ventilation, controls, and equipment

Rules:

- Dry, secure, manufacturer-compliant orientation
- Model and serial labels visible
- Installation kits stored with the associated unit or linked kit location

### GLZ — Padded upright glass area

Purpose:

- Shower glass, mirrors, and specialty glass

Rules:

- Padded upright rack
- Fabrication ID, dimensions, and handing visible
- Restricted impact path

### STG — Project staging bays

Purpose:

- Materials that have completed receiving and are grouped for project release

Rules:

- One clearly labeled bay or subdivided bay per project
- Group by room, trade, and installation phase
- Only accepted materials
- Staging completion checklist and photo required

### LDO — Field loadout

Purpose:

- Final scan, quantity, condition, and custody transfer

Rules:

- Loadout manifest required
- Installer/driver acknowledgment
- Exceptions recorded before departure

### RTS — Returns and surplus

Purpose:

- Vendor returns, project surplus, reusable stock, and disposal decisions

Rules:

- Material remains linked to original project and cost history
- Authorized disposition required

## Label structure

Use a large, scannable warehouse label containing:

- Project number
- Client name
- Project address or abbreviated location
- Room/area
- Trade and installation phase
- Material name
- Manufacturer/model/finish
- SKU and barcode/QR code
- Quantity/unit
- Cabinet tag, dye lot, slab, serial, or other identity where applicable
- Storage/handling warning
- Status: Unverified, Accepted, Hold, Staged, Issued, Return
- Planned start and required-on-site date

Recommended location code pattern:

`ZONE-RACK-BAY-BIN`

Examples:

- `CAB-R02-B03`
- `STN-AF01-S04`
- `SEC-C01-B12`
- `STG-P26041-KIT-CAB`

## Visual management

- **Green:** Accepted
- **Yellow:** Inspection or document pending
- **Red:** Hold/claim/rejected
- **Blue:** Project staged
- **Purple:** High-value/secure

The digital system remains the system of record. Physical colors and labels are visual controls, not substitutes for transactions.

## Daily warehouse discipline

At the start of day:

- Review deliveries, unloading needs, blocked starts, claims, and urgent requests
- Confirm receiving and staging capacity

At the end of day:

- Ensure no unidentified material remains in receiving
- Reconcile exceptions and photos
- Confirm high-value and critical-path locations
- Review tomorrow’s deliveries and claim deadlines
