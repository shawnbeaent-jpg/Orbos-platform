# Category Inspection Templates

These templates define the minimum receiving questions Claude should implement as configurable database-driven checklists. Organizations may add fields, but must not remove mandatory identity, condition, evidence, and exception controls without an administrator-level policy change and audit record.

## Custom cabinetry and finished millwork

Verify:

- Project, room, elevation, cabinet tag, and current design revision
- Manufacturer, collection, door style, species, finish, color, sheen, and construction type
- Cabinet dimensions, configuration, left/right handing, finished ends, and appliance-panel requirements
- Carton count and cabinet/unit count
- Doors, drawers, boxes, frames, finished panels, fillers, toe kicks, moldings, skins, shelves, hardware, and accessories
- Scratches, dents, finish defects, water exposure, warping, broken joints, loose components, and crushed packaging
- Climate-controlled, padded, dry, no-stack storage
- Photos of every cabinet tag and any finish comparison or exception

Blocking examples:

- Wrong finish or door style
- Missing panel, filler, molding, or hardware package
- Duplicate or missing cabinet tag
- Water damage, crushed box, warped component, or visible finish damage
- Storage outside the approved millwork area

## Countertop, natural stone, quartz, and porcelain slab

Verify:

- Material type, manufacturer, color, finish, thickness, edge intent, and approved slab-selection record
- Bundle/lot and slab number
- Slab dimensions and quantity
- Vein direction, book-match or sequence requirements, and approved slab photos
- Chips, cracks, fissures, resin repairs, scratches, staining, and edge damage
- Upright/A-frame handling and restraint
- Fabricated-piece labels, room allocation, cutouts, backsplashes, and edge profiles when receiving fabrication

Blocking examples:

- Wrong slab or bundle
- Damage affecting fabrication yield or visible finished area
- Incorrect thickness, finish, or dimensions
- Flat or unsupported storage when upright/A-frame is required

## Tile and mosaic

Verify:

- Manufacturer, collection, color, finish, size, thickness, rectified/nonrectified status, and current selection
- Dye lot, shade, caliber, batch, carton count, square footage, and overage
- Broken tile count and packaging moisture damage
- Matching trim, bullnose, mosaics, base, corners, and specialty pieces
- Room allocation by lot when multiple approved lots exist
- Keep-dry pallet storage and stacking limits

Blocking examples:

- Mixed dye lot, shade, or caliber without written approval
- Missing trim or specialty pieces
- Insufficient overage
- Breakage reducing usable square footage below required quantity

## Plumbing fixtures, rough-ins, and trim

Verify:

- Manufacturer, collection, model, finish, dimensions, and current selection
- Rough-in valve family and trim compatibility
- Complete component set, cartridges, drains, mounting kits, supply parts, remote valves, hand showers, hoses, and accessories
- Left/right or deck/wall configuration
- Finish damage, dents, cracks, glazing defects, and missing protective film
- Secure storage for high-value trim and protected oversize storage for tubs and sinks

Blocking examples:

- Correct finish but incompatible rough-in
- Missing trim component, drain, mounting kit, or accessory
- Wrong finish, model, configuration, or dimensions
- Cracked fixture or damaged decorative finish

## Appliances and ventilation

Verify:

- Manufacturer, model, serial, finish, voltage, gas/electric configuration, dimensions, and door swing
- Panel-ready or exposed-finish configuration
- Required panels, handles, trim kits, installation kits, cords, hoses, filters, and accessories
- Packaging condition, tip/shock indicators, dents, scratches, glass, and door alignment
- Warranty and installation documents
- Secure, dry, manufacturer-compliant storage

Blocking examples:

- Wrong model, voltage, door swing, or panel-ready configuration
- Duplicate or missing serial number
- Missing installation kit or required accessory
- Packaging damage requiring concealed inspection

## Shower glass, mirrors, and specialty glass

Verify:

- Opening/room, fabrication revision, glass type, thickness, finish, dimensions, handing, swing, and hardware finish
- Etching, coating, privacy treatment, notches, holes, and edgework
- Cracks, chips, scratches, edge damage, and protective packaging
- Upright padded storage and segregation from impact hazards

Blocking examples:

- Wrong fabrication revision, dimensions, handing, or hardware finish
- Edge damage, chip, scratch, or crack
- Flat/unpadded storage when prohibited

## Lighting, controls, and electrical trim

Verify:

- Manufacturer, model, finish, color temperature, lumen output, voltage, wet/damp rating, and current selection
- Driver, transformer, dimmer, control, lamp, trim, and accessory compatibility
- Quantity and room allocation
- Packaging and decorative-finish condition

Blocking examples:

- Wrong color temperature, finish, voltage, wet-location rating, or control compatibility
- Missing driver, transformer, trim, or accessory

## Waterproofing, setting materials, grout, adhesives, sealants, and coatings

Verify:

- Manufacturer system, product, color, batch/lot, quantity, shelf life, expiration, and storage temperature
- Required primers, membranes, tapes, corners, drains, sealants, additives, and accessories
- Packaging integrity, moisture exposure, freezing/heat exposure, and contamination
- Compatibility with specified substrate and system warranty

Blocking examples:

- Expired or temperature-damaged product
- Mixed incompatible manufacturer systems where warranty requires a single system
- Missing required accessory component
- Torn, wet, leaking, or contaminated packaging

## Required evidence rules

For every category, the application must support:

- Overall load photo
- Product-label photo
- BOL/delivery-ticket photo
- Packing-slip photo
- Approved-selection or finish-comparison photo when finish-sensitive
- Storage-location photo
- Exception photos
- Human confirmation of extracted or machine-suggested information
